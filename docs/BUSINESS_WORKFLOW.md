# Business Workflow

## 1. Onboarding
1. User logs in or registers.
2. Company Setup captures legal, tax, branch, and banking data.
3. Invoice Template setup finalizes document defaults.

## 2. Master Data
1. Add Parties (customers/suppliers) with credit controls.
2. Add Items with tax, pricing, and stock configuration.

## 3. Sales Cycle
1. Create Sales Invoice.
2. Validate tax and stock in real time.
3. Track due date, payment status, and balance due.
4. Record Payment In and allocate against open invoices.

## 4. Purchase Cycle
1. Create Purchase Bill.
2. Track payable position and stock increment (including free qty).
3. Record Payment Out and allocate to open bills.

## 5. Monitoring
1. Dashboard surfaces KPIs and AI-style insights.
2. Reports provide filtered export-ready analytics.
3. Notifications/activity logs surface operational events.

## 6. Data Safety
1. Weekly backup export from Backup Utilities.
2. Restore snapshot when needed after confirmation.
